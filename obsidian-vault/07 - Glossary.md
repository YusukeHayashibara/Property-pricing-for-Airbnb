# Glossary

**AISP** — Área Integrada de Segurança Pública. Administrative unit used by ISP-RJ to report crime statistics.

**ITBI** — Imposto sobre Transmissão de Bens Imóveis. Brazilian real-estate transfer tax; its records give geolocated transaction prices.

**Hedonic pricing model** — a regression approach that decomposes a good's price into the value of its constituent characteristics (e.g., bedrooms, location, proximity to POIs).

**Moran's I** — global statistic measuring spatial autocorrelation: whether similar values (e.g., high prices) cluster spatially more than expected by chance.

**LISA (Local Indicators of Spatial Association)** — local version of Moran's I; identifies specific hot spots / cold spots / spatial outliers.

**Microregion** — the spatial unit of analysis used in this project (finer than a full neighborhood, coarser than an individual lot), chosen because ITBI/FipeZAP lack property-level attributes.

**Investment score** — the project's composite output ranking region + property-profile combinations, adjusted for crime risk and tourist appeal.

**PySal** — Python Spatial Analysis Library; used for spatial statistics (Moran/LISA) in this project.
