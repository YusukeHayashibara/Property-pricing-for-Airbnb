# FipeZAP

**Role:** Price validation

## What it provides

Historical residential price index and trend by region, published jointly by FIPE and ZAP.

## How we use it

Cross-check/validate [[ITBI - Data.Rio]] transaction prices and capture price trend over time, since ITBI is transaction-level and can be noisy at fine granularity.
